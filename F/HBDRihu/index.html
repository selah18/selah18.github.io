Happy Birthday, Rihuye. ❤️✈️🩺
For my Rihuye ❤️

Happy birthday, my Rihuye.

Today is about celebrating you, but somehow I feel like I’m the lucky one because your birthday gave the world the person who became such a beautiful part of my life.

I still think about how unexpectedly we met, how one simple hello turned into conversations, dates, laughs, hugs, kisses, and somehow… into us. ❤️ Looking back, it feels almost impossible that something so beautiful could start from such a small moment. And now, having you in my life, I honestly can’t imagine my days without your smile, your little expressions, your beautiful heart, and the peace you bring me.

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel special. A walk, a meal together, sitting somewhere and talking, watching a movie, going to a museum, or even just being next to each other doing absolutely nothing — with you, I never feel like I need anything more.

I love your genuineness. I love the way you care. I love your smile and those adorable cheeks that I can never resist. 😭❤️ I love the way you make me feel understood, loved, and at peace. And more than anything, I love the person I get to be when I’m with you.

You once told me that I feel like peace to you. I hope you know that you are that for me too. In a life that can sometimes be busy, exhausting, and unpredictable, you are one of the places my heart feels at home.

I know I’m not perfect. Sometimes my work gets in the way, sometimes I have to leave, sometimes I can’t give you as much time as I wish I could. But one thing I never want you to question is how much you mean to me. Distance, busy schedules, flights, long days — none of those things change what I feel for you.

On your birthday, I don’t just wish you happiness for today. I wish you a life filled with everything your heart deserves. I want to see you achieve your dreams, become even more of the incredible woman you are, laugh until your stomach hurts, travel, discover beautiful places, eat all the chocolate and ice cream you want 😂❤️, and have countless moments that make you genuinely happy.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be there for many of them.

I want more birthdays with you. More dates. More random walks. More museum days. More movies. More hugs that last longer than they should. More kisses. More inside jokes. More little arguments that we laugh about later. More memories that one day we’ll look back on and say, “Remember when…”

And hopefully, one day, we’ll look back at this birthday and realize it was just one of the many birthdays we celebrated together.

Rihuye, thank you for coming into my life and making it softer, happier, and more beautiful. Thank you for loving me, for understanding me, for believing in us, and simply for being you.

I don’t know exactly what every chapter of our future will look like, but I know who I want beside me while I discover it.

Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. ❤️

May this new year of your life bring you closer to everything you’ve ever dreamed of. And may I have the privilege of being beside you through as much of it as possible.

I love you more than I can properly put into words.

Happy birthday, Rihuye. ❤️🎂
